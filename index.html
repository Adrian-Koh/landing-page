Designing a good outfit is simple

It doesn't take much to look well put together. A clean looking outfit doesn't cost much and needs only a few good elements.

Essential items

A good fitting t-shirt goes a long way. Go for basic colors such as black or white to get the effortless yet clean look.

A pair of high quality jeans which are tailored to fit your leg length and shape beats any of the trendy pants that are out there.

You can never go wrong with white sneakers. Make sure you're always cleaning them to ensure they appear fresh.

Accessories such as a necklace and rings adds a lot to a basic outfit. Play around with different accessories to find your style.
